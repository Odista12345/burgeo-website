Burgeo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Burgeo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of families. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Burgeo - Parently app, Burgeo Kids app, and related services (the "Services").

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Parent Account: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Family Information: Family name, family code
  • Child Profiles: First name, last name (optional), date of birth, gender, grade level, profile photo (optional)

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, app version, device type
  • Usage Data: Screen time minutes, app usage categories, daily usage logs
  • Pairing Information: Device pairing codes and connection status

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not scan or store the content of your child's messages, social media posts, or browsing history
  • We do not sell any personal information
  • We do not display third-party advertising

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage parent accounts
  • Enable family and child profile management
  • Track screen time and enforce usage limits set by parents
  • Generate weekly reports and usage analytics for parents
  • Provide device pairing and synchronization
  • Send alerts and notifications (with parent consent)
  • Improve and troubleshoot the Services

4. Data Storage and Security

Privacy-First Architecture: Child activity data is encrypted and stored locally on the parent's device whenever possible. Our cloud stores only authentication credentials, subscription metadata, encrypted sync payloads, and device pairing metadata.

Security Measures: Passwords hashed with bcrypt, HTTPS transmission, JWT authentication, role-based access controls.

5. Data Ownership and Control

Parents have full control over family data. Use the Family Vault in the app to export, backup, restore, transfer, or delete data at any time.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Burgeo complies with COPPA. Parents provide consent by creating child profiles. Children cannot create accounts independently. Child data is only accessible to the parent account that created it.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell personal information, share data with advertisers, or allow third parties to access child usage data. We may share with service providers under confidentiality or when required by law.

8. Data Retention

Usage data retained for 12 months by default. Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

If in the EEA, you have rights to access, correct, delete, export, and object to processing of your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
