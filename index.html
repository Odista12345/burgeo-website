Frequently Asked Questions

How does screen time tracking work?

Burgeo tracks usage on your child's device and syncs it to your parent dashboard. When limits are reached, the device locks automatically.

Can I set different rules for each child?

Yes! Each child has their own profile with individual screen time limits, bedtime schedules, and wellness break settings.

Is my family data private?

Absolutely. Your data stays on your device. We only store essential account and payment information on our servers.

What devices are supported?

Burgeo works on Android devices. iOS support is coming soon.
